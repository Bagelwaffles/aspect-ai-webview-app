# Fiverr Quick Marketing Audit — Fulfillment Playbook

## Channel rule

This playbook applies only to Quick Marketing Audit orders originating on Fiverr.

Fiverr remains the order, payment, buyer-message, revision, deadline, and delivery source of truth. Do not direct Fiverr buyers to AMS Stripe checkout, email payment links, or another communication channel to complete the Fiverr order.

## Offer package

The Fiverr order receives:

1. Five marketing problems
2. Five specific fixes
3. An improved headline
4. An improved offer
5. One ready-to-use promotional post
6. A practical seven-day action plan

Use the package and delivery terms shown on the live Fiverr Gig/custom offer for the specific order. Do not silently expand scope.

## Order intake

Before work begins, the operator must verify inside Fiverr:

- the order exists and is active
- requirements have been submitted when required
- the delivery deadline shown by Fiverr
- the exact package/custom-offer scope
- any attachments or buyer instructions that are necessary to fulfill the order

Notification email is an alert source, not the final source of truth for order state.

## Required buyer information

For the Quick Marketing Audit, collect only what is needed for the purchased service:

- business name
- website, Facebook page, or other public business URL
- product or service
- ideal customer
- biggest marketing problem
- desired result

If a required item is missing, draft a concise Fiverr message asking only for the missing information. The operator sends it manually in Fiverr.

## Fulfillment sequence

1. Verify the active order and deadline in Fiverr.
2. Confirm the purchased scope.
3. Read the submitted requirements and permitted attachments.
4. Review the buyer's public-facing business information relevant to the audit.
5. Capture specific public evidence for each marketing problem.
6. Draft the audit using the structure below.
7. Check every recommendation against the public evidence and buyer requirements.
8. Review tone, scope, spelling, claims, and usability.
9. Prepare the Fiverr delivery message and audit attachment/text.
10. Operator manually opens Fiverr and delivers the completed work.
11. Record that delivery occurred only after Fiverr confirms the submission.
12. If the buyer requests a revision, prepare a revision draft within the purchased revision scope and require operator approval again.

## Audit drafting prompt

```text
You are assisting Aspect Marketing Solutions with a Fiverr Quick Marketing Audit.

Order rules:
- This is a Fiverr-originated order.
- Do not ask the buyer to pay or communicate outside Fiverr.
- Do not claim access to private analytics, revenue, ad accounts, customer records, or internal business data unless the buyer explicitly supplied that information for this order.
- Do not invent testimonials, results, rankings, conversion rates, revenue, or guarantees.

Buyer/business information:
- Business name: [BUSINESS NAME]
- Public business URL: [URL]
- Product/service: [PRODUCT OR SERVICE]
- Ideal customer: [IDEAL CUSTOMER]
- Biggest marketing problem: [CHALLENGE]
- Desired result: [DESIRED RESULT]

Public observations:
[PASTE SPECIFIC PUBLIC OBSERVATIONS]

Produce exactly these sections:

1. Executive Snapshot
   - 3-5 sentences.

2. Five Marketing Problems
   - Numbered 1-5.
   - Each problem must tie to a specific public observation.

3. Five Specific Fixes
   - Numbered 1-5.
   - Each fix maps directly to the matching problem.

4. Improved Headline
   - One primary headline.
   - Optional one-sentence subheadline.

5. Improved Offer
   - Clarify target customer, value, what is included, and CTA.
   - No guaranteed financial outcomes.

6. One Promotional Post
   - Ready to use.
   - Natural voice.
   - One clear CTA.
   - No fake scarcity or fabricated social proof.

7. Seven-Day Action Plan
   - Day 1 through Day 7.
   - One or two concrete actions per day.

8. Priority Summary
   - Top three actions to do first.

Tone: practical, direct, specific, professional. Avoid filler and generic marketing advice.
```

## Fiverr delivery draft

Use this as a starting point, then customize it to the order:

```text
Thanks for your order. I completed your Quick Marketing Audit using the business information you provided and the public marketing material available for review.

Your audit includes:
- 5 marketing problems
- 5 specific fixes
- an improved headline
- an improved offer
- 1 promotional post
- a 7-day action plan

I focused on specific, practical changes rather than generic advice. The completed audit is included with this delivery.

If something in the purchased scope needs clarification, use the Fiverr order conversation and I’ll review it there.
```

Do not include an AMS Stripe link, external payment request, external contact request, or unrelated upsell in the delivery message.

## Revision handling

- Read the buyer's revision request inside Fiverr before changing the deliverable.
- Compare the request with the purchased package and included revisions.
- Fix in-scope issues directly.
- If the request materially changes the original scope, do not argue or auto-accept. Draft a neutral response for operator review using Fiverr's available order tools.
- Never submit a revision automatically.

## Deadline handling

Treat the Fiverr order page as the authoritative deadline. Email-derived deadline parsing is only an alert convenience.

If the bridge reports an urgent deadline:

1. open the Fiverr order immediately
2. verify the actual timer/deadline
3. prioritize completion or use Fiverr's supported order-resolution tools if a legitimate issue blocks delivery

Do not fabricate progress or submit incomplete work solely to stop a timer.

## Cancellation / dispute handling

If the notification indicates cancellation, dispute, or order-status trouble, stop fulfillment automation and inspect the Fiverr order manually. Do not deliver after an order is confirmed canceled. Do not attempt to collect replacement payment outside Fiverr.

## Human approval gate

Before every Fiverr-facing message, revision, or delivery, the operator checks:

- correct buyer/order
- correct package scope
- correct deadline
- no invented facts
- no unsupported performance claims
- no private-data assumptions
- no off-platform payment/contact request
- deliverable is complete and useful

Only then should the operator perform the action manually in Fiverr.
