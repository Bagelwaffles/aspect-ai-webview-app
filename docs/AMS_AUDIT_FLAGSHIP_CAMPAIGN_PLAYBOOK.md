## Executive decision
Make the **$49 Quick Marketing Audit** AMS's flagship low-friction *customer-acquisition* offer. The business is not the $49 invoice alone: it is the optional **done-for-you implementation**, then appropriate ongoing marketing management or AMS SaaS self-service. Audit always remains fully valuable on its own; no forced upsell.

Positioning: **"Find what is holding your marketing back. See the evidence. Choose what to fix yourself—or have AMS implement it."**
Secondary line: **"Your marketing audit. Your way."**

Never assert that unshipped features, checkout availability, video formats, testimonials, integrations, or implementation services are live. The website currently contains a runtime-gated checkout, structured audit result view, HTML download and browser print/PDF. Format selection, personalized video and implementation-order workflow require development and quality proof.

## Evidence-based differentiation
Public competitors currently span free, $49, $197, $500 and $999+ audits; price alone is not a defensible moat. AMS must compete on specificity, evidence, explanation, personal format choice, and the approved ability to **complete** the work.

## Ideal customer profile and exclusions
**First 30 days:** Kentucky/US owner-operated *local service businesses* that already have a public website or business page, can make purchase decisions, and need more inquiries. Prioritize (a) home/repair services, (b) salons/wellness providers for general marketing (no health claims), and (c) local professional services. Start with one vertical until fulfillment data supports expansion.

Qualify by: public URL, business type, one primary goal, main service, target area, current CTA, willingness to make a website or marketing change. Reject requests for fake reviews, deceptive guarantees, unverifiable ranking claims, automated spam, unauthorized account access or scope far larger than a $49 public-facing audit.

## Customer journey and lifecycle
**Visit → sample proof → consent-checked capture (optional) → secure audit checkout → evidence-based audit → result ready → choose DIY or request implementation quote → quote/SOW → explicit approval → fulfillment → customer acceptance → optional ongoing management.**

- Pre-purchase: a sample audit/short video; FAQs; no bait-and-switch.
- Abandoned checkout: only send permitted follow-up with independently verified consent and correct sender/opt-out compliance; never infer opt-in from entering an email.
- Receipt/delivery: transactional status only, keep marketing separate. Existing draft email PR #282 remains HOLD.
- Day 0 delivery: customer chooses how to consume the report; one-click select fixes for a quote.
- Day +2: optional value check-in through a channel the customer agreed to.
- Day +7: progress check with helpful DIY guidance; offer implementation only as separate option.
- Day +21 or +30: optional ongoing review for customers who elected marketing communications.
- Fiverr: **keep all Fiverr orders, communications, payment and revisions on Fiverr**, never solicit off-platform transactions.

## Implementation packages — scope discipline
**Quick Fix, from $149, proposed:** one bounded CTA, headline, on-page metadata or profile section. Customer supplies editor access through scoped legitimate collaborator invitation where necessary.
**Growth Sprint, from $499, proposed:** up to three discrete agreed improvements; define deliverables and revision caps.
**Full Implementation, from $999, proposed:** address up to five findings *only within an individually written SOW*; no unlimited rebuild or assumed platform access.
**Ongoing Growth Management:** discovery and quote; marketing management/ads require budget and approval explicitly separate from service fees.
For every job: estimated complexity, prerequisites, milestones, third-party costs, security, deadline, cancellation/refund, change requests, evidence of completion, owner approval before customer-facing publishing or spend. Quote before payment; no automatic charge from a "fix it for me" click.

**Wave B: partnerships**
- Designers, freelance web developers, local chambers/merchant groups, IT consultants, legitimate creators and adjacent agencies.
- Use existing /collaborate route where relevant. Draft referral terms with conversion attribution, anti-fraud, refund clawback, payment schedule and FTC relationship disclosure before turning on commissions.

**Wave C: ads** only after verified conversion tracking, at least a usable unit economics sample, clean delivery SLA, privacy/cookie consent where required, and a budget explicitly approved by owner.

## Measurement / experiment plan
Existing site code has `components/marketing-attribution.tsx` and `app/api/marketing/attribution/route.ts` for UTM campaign fields. Reuse them; do **not** assert they currently reconcile with purchase/quote/fulfillment. Add privacy-reviewed, event-ID-based reconciliation.
Events: `audit_page_view`, `sample_view`, `intake_started`, `checkout_started`, `paid_audit_confirmed`, `audit_ready`, `audit_delivered`, `implementation_quote_requested`, `quote_accepted`, `implementation_paid`, `implementation_completed`, `retainer_started`.
Use explicit definitions and deduping; purchases only count from verified paid Stripe events, not pageviews or thank-you URL loads. Avoid storing private customer URL, session token, email or raw report in analytics.

| Metric | Formula / definition | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Landing conversion | verified audit payments / unique eligible audit landing sessions | acquisition health |
| Audit delivery SLA | paid audits delivered within promised SLA / paid audits due | fulfillment truth |
| Audit cost | actual reviewer hours × loaded rate + model/hosting/media + transaction fee | unit economics |
| Gross margin | collected revenue - direct cost and refunds | scale/no-scale |
| Quote request rate | unique paid-audit customers requesting quote / delivered audits | upsell interest |
| Quote acceptance | approved SOWs / valid quotes | offer fit |
| Implementation margin | implementation receipts - direct labor, tool, platform, contractor and refunds | profitability |
| Refund/dispute rate | refunded/disputed payments / verified payments | trust |
| Referral conversion | verified eligible paid orders / attributed referred visits | partner performance |
| Retainer attach | signed continuing customers / completed implementation customers | retention |

**Capacity brake:** Before paid traffic, measure human review and fix effort on a small, consented pilot. If cost or 48h SLA fails, reduce included scope, raise price, hire capacity, or pause checkout; do not silently miss delivery.

**Experiments, one variable at a time:** Hero "five evidence-backed fixes" vs "stop losing qualified inquiries"; show sample report near top vs below price; verticalized proof vs general messaging; CTA "See a sample" vs "Get my audit". Run only with real traffic, stable measurement and a predeclared stop rule.

## Governance, privacy, and operational gates
- Never invent metrics, before/after results, audit scores, screenshots, customer testimonials or permissions.
- Public evidence screenshots must be rights-appropriate and clearly contextualized. Private analytics use customer-approved least-privilege OAuth/invites, never passwords in forms/chat.
- Paid customer reports remain private, retention-bound and order-verified; no data leaks in preview/social/video.
- Implementations that publish, spend, modify customer accounts, contact recipients or create third-party charges need specific customer approval. Internal owner approval required for activating payments, paid ads, external posting, email or production changes.
- Existing social-publishing approval-first control, marketing attribution, audit fulfillment, and Fiverr channel rules are reused.
- Commercial emails: accurate sender and subject, address, unsubscribe/opt-out compliance and consent practices; do not launch email sends until current Gmail delivery gates pass.
- FTC: never fabricate/incentivize favorable reviews, misrepresent "independent" examples, hide partner compensation, or claim proof of outcomes without evidence.

## Phases and release criteria
**Phase 0 — delivery-proof gate:** Readiness check for live checkout, paid order → native audit result, private results, 48h SLA, refund state and email/manual delivery. No live campaign traffic if blocked.
**Phase 1 — offer/proof:** Implement honest benefit-led landing copy, synthetic sample report, FAQ, source links, scoped formats. Run mobile, accessibility, security and conversion QA.
**Phase 2 — upgrades:** Customer-selected findings to nonbinding quote request with durable records, manual owner review. No implementation execution without accepted SOW/payment and authorized access.
**Phase 3 — owned-channel launch:** Publish approved posts/video, setup consented nurture and partner interest, use UTM tracking and honest creative. No ads until margin evidence.
**Phase 4 — controlled scale:** Reconcile orders/fulfillment/events, test one landing variable, pilot vetted referral partners, consider paid channels only with approved cap and retention evidence.
