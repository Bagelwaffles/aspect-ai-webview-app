CTA: Link to audit sample/offer only after verified production release; use captions, transcript, descriptive title and direct canonical URL.

## 9. Buyer objections and truthful responses
**"Why should I pay if free audits exist?"**
"Some free audits are excellent. AMS focuses on a concise, actionable report specific to the site you submit, with prioritized next steps and the option to request help implementing them. Compare our sample before buying."

**"Will this guarantee more leads?"**
"No. We explain observable weaknesses, practical fixes and how to measure results. We cannot guarantee performance."

**"Do you need my passwords?"**
"No. A public-facing audit begins with the URL and business goals. Implementation access, where required, uses individually approved collaborator or delegated permissions."

**"Can you do all the fixes for $149?"**
"No. The suggested starting price is for one bounded quick fix. We quote the real scope before any additional payment."

**"Will you automatically sign me up for a subscription?"**
"No. The $49 audit is one time. Implementation and management are separate, opt-in purchases."

**"Can I get a video?"**
"Only if the requested format is listed as available at the time of purchase. Personalized narration or video production may require a separately priced scope."

**"How fast?"**
"The current audit offer states 48 hours after verified payment. AMS must validate this service level before accepting campaign-driven volume. Implementation timelines are quoted separately."
